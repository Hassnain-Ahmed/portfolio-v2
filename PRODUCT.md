# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, design/eng leads, potential clients, and fellow developers landing on
Hassnain Ahmed's personal portfolio. They arrive to judge craft and taste in seconds,
often from a link (LinkedIn, GitHub, a referral). Context: quick first impression, then a
short scan of work, process, and about. Mobile and desktop both matter.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for a **full-stack developer** who designs and ships **AI-powered
products and SaaS end to end** (frontend, backend, and everything between) — working solo
but able to outsource. The site itself is the proof of work: the design and interaction
quality ARE the pitch. Success = the visitor immediately reads "this person builds and ships
real, fast, well-crafted AI & SaaS products" and continues to the work. Not a "design
engineer" — a legitimate product builder.

## Brand Personality

Playful, energetic, confident. Warm and human, not corporate. The voice is direct and a
little bold ("I craft digital experiences"). It should feel like a maker who enjoys the
craft — lively motion, warmth, personality — while still reading as precise and fast.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark-purple "AI SaaS" landing pages (the site's inner pages lean dark/purple;
  the hero deliberately breaks warm to stand apart).
- Heavy hero gimmicks that trade first-paint speed for spectacle (the old Spline 3D scene
  blocked the headline for up to 15s — removed).
- Editorial-magazine cosplay (display-serif + italic drop caps + broadsheet grid). Not
  this brand.
- Cold, restrained, beige "tasteful minimalism." This brand is warm and energetic, not quiet.

## Design Principles

- **Fast is a feature.** Every hero effect must paint in milliseconds. Perceived speed is
  part of the craft claim. No effect earns its place if it delays the headline.
- **Warmth carries the brand.** The hero commits to a warm terracotta/peach world; warmth
  comes from color, motion, and copy — never from a washed-out tinted neutral.
- **Playful, but legible.** Motion and interactivity should feel alive and reactive, but
  content is readable on first frame and contrast is never sacrificed for effect.
- **Show, don't tell.** The interface demonstrates skill instead of claiming it. The hero's
  interaction is a live sample of the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Body/subtitle text ≥ 4.5:1, large headings ≥ 3:1 (guaranteed via a
scrim behind text over the animated canvas). Every animation has a
`prefers-reduced-motion` fallback (the canvas renders a single static warm frame; entrance
animations become instant). All interactive elements are real `<a>`/`<button>` with visible
focus states and keyboard reachability.
